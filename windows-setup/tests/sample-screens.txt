  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Go back to Windows Phone
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A) Keep Android on the SD card, but start Windows Phone again (Windows Phone still installed):
       in Android, open a root shell (adb shell, then su) and run:  superman-bootmenu windows
       then restart. Windows Phone starts by default from then on.
       Back to Android: restart while holding Volume Up, then run:  superman-bootmenu android
       (The keys cannot move through the boot menu, so the default entry decides what starts.)

  B) Remove Android completely and get a factory-fresh Windows Phone (works in every case,
     also after Android was moved to the internal storage; tested):
       1. Take the SD card out of the phone.
       2. Open WPinternals. Choose "Flash" on the left, scroll down to "Flash original FFU"
          and pick the stock FFU file of your phone.
       3. Plug the phone into the PC.
       4. Restart the phone: hold Power for about 10 seconds, until it vibrates.
          WPinternals catches the phone while it starts; the "Flash" button becomes available.
       5. Click "Flash". WPinternals shows "Restoring bootloader..." and then flashes the FFU.
       6. Wait for "Flash successful". The phone restarts into the Windows Phone setup.

     This removes Android, the boot shim and the bootloader unlock. Your phone's calibration and
     addresses are kept. Never use the key combination shown on a red "!" screen.

  This tool cannot press the buttons in WPinternals for you; the steps above are all there is.
